# V10 nested evaluation — results

> This platform is intended for academic research and software experimentation only. It has not been clinically validated and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment decisions or emergency medical decisions. Model outputs are experimental predictions and require independent clinical validation.

Generated by `scripts/write_results_report.py` from `results/v10/nested/*/results.json`. Design (`configs/v10/analysis_plan.json`): leave one reconstructed scan unit out (8 outer folds); hyperparameters, Platt calibration and the Youden threshold chosen on grouped inner folds of the training units only; primary metric = unweighted mean of within-unit ROC AUC (tumour-vs-tumour-free pairs inside the held-out unit). Units are reconstructed scan groups, **not patients**. Quantum and hybrid models run on a classical NumPy state-vector simulator; no quantum hardware was used and no quantum advantage is claimed.

## How to read this

* The within-unit AUC asks: inside one held-out scan unit, does the model rank tumour patches above tumour-free patches? The pooled outer-fold AUC mixes units and is lower because score levels shift between units; together with the ECE values this shows the probabilities are not calibrated across units.
* Differences that are not significant are not evidence of equivalence: with 7 qualifying units the smallest attainable two-sided p of the exact sign-flip test is 1/64.
* These estimates are for 96 × 96 liver-tissue patches selected with expert masks, on one public dataset.
