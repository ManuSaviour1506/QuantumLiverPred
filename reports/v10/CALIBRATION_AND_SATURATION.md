# Calibration and saturation report (V10 audit finding 4)

Generated by `scripts/v10_calibration_report.py`. No model, threshold or calibration was changed.

## Why the V9 deep models saturate

V9 calibrates each CNN with Platt scaling on the *validation* scan groups. Those groups are almost perfectly separable (validation AUC 0.997-0.9996, the 'saturated validation set' of V9 Stage 2), so the fitted slope on the standardised score is very steep (column `slope`). A steep slope maps most scores to probabilities near 0 or 1. On the held-out test groups the same models are worse calibrated than on validation (test ECE vs validation ECE) and less specific, so p = 1.000 on a slice must not be read as certainty.

## Consequences in the platform

* Every result carries a `saturated` flag (p >= 0.999) and the model's calibration source; the dashboard shows both.
* The confidence shown is the decision margin from the model's own threshold, not a probability of being correct.
* Generalisation cannot be judged from 8 sample slices: re-run this script and `scripts/evaluate_models.py` on the full dataset (scan-group-level held-out test split).
